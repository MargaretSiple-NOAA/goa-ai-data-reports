Fix the section breaks between portrait and landscape pages. This will prevent lost pages.
Macro for replacing Section Break (odd page) with Section Break (continuous):
            Sub ReplaceOddPageBreaksWithContinuous()
                ' Macro to replace "Odd Page" section breaks with "Continuous" breaks
                ' Works on the active document
                
                Dim sec As Section
                
                ' Loop through each section in the document, starting from the second section
                ' (Section 1 cannot have a break before it)
                For Each sec In ActiveDocument.Sections
                    If sec.PageSetup.SectionStart = wdSectionOddPage Then
                        sec.PageSetup.SectionStart = wdSectionContinuous
                    End If
                Next sec
                
                MsgBox "All 'Odd Page' section breaks have been changed to 'Continuous'."
            End Sub
            
            
Open your Word document and press Alt + F11 to open the VBA Editor.
Click Insert > Module.
Paste the code above into the module window.
Close the VBA Editor and press Alt + F8, select ReplaceOddPageBreaksWithContinuous, and click Run.
